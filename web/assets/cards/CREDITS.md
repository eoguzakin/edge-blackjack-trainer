# Traditional playing cards

Original SVG artwork by Adrian Kennard: https://www.me.uk/cards/

Released by the creator under CC0 1.0 Universal. Full dedication: LICENSE-CC0.txt.

Downloaded 2026-10-04 using the creator's standard two-color deck and Plain Ace option:
https://www.me.uk/cards/makeadeck.cgi?zip=1&ace=Plain&ace1=&ace2=&qr=

SVG files are unchanged. Court cards are based on traditional Goodall & Son designs.
Names are rank + suit: A, 2–9, T (ten), J, Q, K; C, D, H, S.
1B is the black back, 2B the red back. The native aspect ratio is 5:7.
